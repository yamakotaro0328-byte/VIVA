2026.10.18

Events for October–December announced Notice

Our official events through the end of the year are now on the event calendar.

🎃 Candy Kingdom Harvest Festival (10/24–11/1) — gather ingredients, run candy workshops and deliver to your national treasury, plus the candy post and a candy house contest. Opening ceremony 10/24 at 20:00; on 10/31 at 21:00 every nation takes on the Great Pumpkin King; voting and awards 11/1 at 20:00. Candy can be exchanged until 11/8.

🏅 VIVA Sports Day (11/21–11/23, three-day weekend) — nations send representatives to compete in a boat race, speed building, an elytra race, spleef and a PvP tournament. Entries open 11/8–11/18.

🎄 Winter Trade Fair (12/19–12/25) — every nation runs a stall at the snowfield market, plus a Santa delivery quest to other nations. Gift exchange 12/24 at 21:00. Stall applications close 12/12.

🏆 Annual awards and New Year (12/27, 12/31) — vote 12/20–12/26 for the Annual Nation Awards on 12/27 (largest territory, most trade, best build and more). Countdown fireworks and a New Year group photo from 23:00 on 12/31.

Dates may change. Details will be announced on Discord. You can also add the calendar to your phone.
