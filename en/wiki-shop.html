QUICKSHOP-HIKARI

Open a chest shop

Just place a chest and anyone can open an automatic shop. It can also buy items from others.

Selling / buying

Just place a chest and anyone can open an automatic shop.

Sell items

  • Place a chest
  • Hold the item you want to sell and left-click the chest
  • Type the price per item in chat
  • Put items in the chest and others can buy them automatically

Buy items from others

  • Look at your shop's chest and run /qs mode buy
  • It becomes a buying shop at the same price
  • No need to put money in the chest. Payments come from your own money
  • Items you buy go into the chest, so leave some space

Things to know

A buying shop stops when you run out of money. Payments come from your own money, not the chest, so it can't buy once your balance runs low. The same happens when the chest is full.

Commands

Run all of these while looking at the chest shop.

/qs price <price>Change the item price
/qs mode buySwitch to buying mode
/qs mode sellSwitch to selling mode
/qs removeRemove a shop
