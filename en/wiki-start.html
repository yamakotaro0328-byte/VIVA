What should I do in my first week?

No need to found a nation right away. Get used to things one step at a time.

  1. 1

    Get a job

    Choose a job with /jobs browse. The things you normally do turn into money. See Earn with a job.

  2. 2

    Join an existing town

    Before founding your own nation, join an existing town and get used to earning money and how land works.

  3. 3

    Claim your own land

    Claim the area around your bed with /claim for peace of mind. See Protecting land & founding nations.

  4. 4

    Check out the shops

    Buy and sell the materials you need at chest shops and villager shops. See Open a chest shop.

  5. 5

    Found a nation once you have money and friends

    Write a constitution and laws, and start diplomacy and trade. Take your time and go at your own pace.

If you need help

Ask anything on Discord. If you see trouble, report it with /report. All the rules are on the Rules page.
