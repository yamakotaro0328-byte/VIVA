Homes & teleport

Setting homes, going back to spawn, and teleporting to other players.

Set a home

Your own return point that takes you back to base in an instant. Name them to register several.

/sethome <name>Set your current location as a home
/home <name>Teleport to that home (costs money)
/homesList your homes
/delhome <name>Delete a home

Go back to spawn

Use /spawn to go back to the server's starting point. This costs money too.

Go to another player

There are two kinds. Who pays depends on who moves.

/tpa — you go to them

  • Send a request with /tpa <player>
  • When they accept, you go to them
  • You're the one moving, so you pay

/tphere — bring them to you

  • Send a request with /tphere <player>
  • When they accept, they come to you
  • They're the one moving, so they pay (collect on delivery)

Accept a request by clicking the button in chat, typing the same command again, or using /accept (or /ok, which is easy to type on Bedrock).

About fees

/home, /spawn, /tpa and /tphere all cost more the farther you actually travel. Short trips are cheap, and there's a minimum fee.

After a teleport starts, you need to stay still for a moment. Moving your view is fine, but walking, having hostile mobs nearby, or having just fought another player cancels it. If it's cancelled, you aren't charged.

Use the menu

No need to memorize commands: /menu opens a click menu.

Commands

/sethome <name>Set a home
/home <name>Teleport to a home
/homesList your homes
/delhome <name>Delete a home
/spawnTeleport to spawn
/tpa <player>Request to go to someone (you pay)
/tphere <player>Request to bring someone to you (they pay)
/tpaccept / /accept / /okAccept a request
/tpdenyDecline a request
/tpacancelCancel a request you sent
/menuOpen the click menu
