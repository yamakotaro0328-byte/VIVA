CHEAT SHEET

Commands

Every command that appears in this Wiki, in one place. Open this whenever you're stuck.

Jobs Reborn — jobs

See Earning with jobs for details.

/jobs browseOpen the list of jobs you can take; join from here
/jobs join <job>Take the named job
/jobs leave <job>Quit that job (your level may reset)
/jobs statsCheck your jobs, levels and XP to the next level
/jobs info <job>See what a job pays and for what

Lands — land & nations

See Protecting land for details.

/landsOpen the main menu (UI)
/claimCreate a land in your name and claim the chunk you're standing in
/land <land> claim radius <radius>Claim the surrounding chunks at once
/land <land> unclaimUnclaim the chunk you're standing in
/land <land> member add <player>Add as a member
/land <land> member remove <player>Remove a member
/land <land> chatToggle land chat on/off

Build server (WorldGuard)

See Build server for details.

/server buildGo to the build server
//wandGet the selection tool (wooden axe)
/rg define <region>Protect the selected area
/rg addmember <region> <player>Add a member
/rg addowner <region> <player>Add an owner
/rg info <region>See region info
/rg listList your regions
/rg flag <region> <flag> <value>Change detailed settings

Diplomacy & war

See Diplomacy & war for details.

/nations create <land> [nation name]Found a nation with that land as its capital
/land <land> relation ally add <target>Request an alliance
/land <land> relation enemy add <target>Mark as an enemy (required to declare war)
/land <land> war declare <target>Declare war
/land <land> war menuCheck the war status or surrender

Economy

See Economy & money for details.

/balanceCheck your money (EcoTP)
/pay <player> <amount>Send money (EcoTP)
/baltopSee the richest players (EcoTP)
/land <land> bank balanceSee a land's bank balance
/land <land> bank deposit <amount>Deposit money into the bank
/lands eco taxesCheck upcoming taxes
/lands eco upkeepCheck upcoming upkeep

Homes & teleport

See Homes & teleport for details.

/sethome <name>Set a home
/home <name>Teleport to a home
/homesList your homes
/delhome <name>Delete a home
/spawnTeleport to spawn
/tpa <player>Request to go to someone (you pay)
/tphere <player>Request to bring someone to you (they pay)
/accept / /okAccept a request
/tpdenyDecline a request
/menuOpen the click menu

QuickShop-Hikari — chest shops

Run these while looking at the chest shop. See Chest shops for details.

/qs price <price>Change the item price
/qs mode buySwitch to buying mode (no need to put money in the chest)
/qs mode sellSwitch to selling mode
/qs removeRemove a shop

Villager shops

See Villager shops for details.

/shopkeeper removeRemove a Shopkeepers villager shop

Casino

The casino (GambleGate) needs no commands; just sit on a chair or press a button to play. See Playing at the casino for details.
