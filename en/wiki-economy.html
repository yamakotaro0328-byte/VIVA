ECONOMY

Economy & money overview

Where to start earning, plus land banks, taxes, upkeep and renting.

How to earn money

There are three main ways to earn. Each has its own page.

Earn with a jobJobs Reborn. Mining, chopping and fighting turn straight into money
Open a chest shopQuickShop-Hikari. Both buying and selling
Trade with villagersShopkeepers and Villager Market

Your money (wallet)

The server currency is managed by a plugin called EcoTP. Money you earn is stored here and used for buying and selling in shops and for home and teleport fees.

/balanceCheck your current money
/pay <player> <amount>Send money to another player
/baltopSee the richest players

We moved over from Essentials, but your money carried over as is. There's nothing to re-check.

Land banks

Each land has a bank where money can be stored. Taxes and upkeep are paid from here first.

/land <land> bank balanceSee the bank balance
/land <land> bank deposit <amount>Deposit money into the bank
/land <land> bank withdraw <amount>Withdraw money from the bank (requires permission)

Taxes and upkeep

Living on a land may cost taxes, and owning a land may cost upkeep. If the bank balance runs short, protection may be removed, so be careful.

/lands eco taxesCheck taxes you'll be paying
/lands eco upkeepCheck upkeep you'll be paying
If upkeep can't be paid, part of your protection may be removed. Keep plenty of money in your land's bank.

Cost of claiming

Claiming chunks (/claim) may cost money. Unclaiming (/land <land> unclaim) may refund part of it.

Renting and selling

You can rent out or sell part of your land to other players.

/lands rental listList lands for rent or sale
/lands rental infoSee rental/sale info for where you're standing
/lands rental cancelEnd a rental contract you're renting yourself

To rent out land, place a sign or set it up from /lands menu here. Ask on Discord for details.
