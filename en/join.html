Join

Welcome to VIVA-MC. Here's how to connect and what you'll need.

How to join on your device

Pick your device to see only the steps for it.

Joining from a PC (Java Edition)

  1. Launch Minecraft Java Edition 26.2.
  2. On the title screen, choose "Multiplayer" → "Add Server".
  3. In "Server Address", enter (no port needed).
  4. Click "Done", select VIVA-MC in the list and click "Join Server".

Set "Server Resource Packs" to Enabled so vehicles and more look right.

Joining from a phone or tablet (Bedrock)

  1. Update Minecraft to the latest version, launch it and tap "Play".
  2. Open "Servers" from the tabs at the top and tap "Add Server" at the very bottom.
  3. Enter VIVA-MC as the server name, the address , and port 19132.
  4. Tap "Save", then connect to VIVA-MC from the list.

Long-press to paste the address. The button above copies it.

Joining from Windows (Bedrock)

  1. Update Minecraft (Bedrock) to the latest version, launch it and click "Play".
  2. Open the "Servers" tab and click "Add Server".
  3. Enter VIVA-MC as the server name, the address , and port 19132.
  4. Tap "Save", then connect to VIVA-MC from the list.

If your Minecraft icon says "Java Edition", follow the PC (Java Edition) steps instead.

Joining from Switch / PS / Xbox

Console versions of Minecraft don't let you add outside servers from the menu. Joining takes an extra step, such as changing your console's network settings.

The steps differ by console and change from time to time, so tell us your console on Discord and we'll explain the method that works now.

Ask how to join on Discord

If you couldn't get in, check server status to see if it's open, or look for a temporary address.

Steps to connect

Follow these steps from the top and you're in.

  1. 1

    Check your version

    You can join from either Java Edition or Bedrock Edition. Please use the latest stable version.

  2. 2

    Add the server

    On the Multiplayer screen, choose "Add Server" and enter the address below.

  3. 3

    Once inside, head to a town

    No need to found a nation right away. Join an existing town first and get used to earning money and how land works.

  4. 4

    Found your own nation

    Once you have money and friends, you can found a nation. Write a constitution and laws, and start diplomacy and trade.
