NOVA-FARMERSDELIGHT

Cooking & farming

Nova-FarmersDelight: content that expands cooking and farming (not available on Bedrock).

Bedrock Edition isn't supported for now. The required resource pack is large and can't be delivered to Bedrock players yet. We plan to add Bedrock support step by step; until then, please enjoy it on Java Edition. We'll announce it in the news when it's ready.

What is Nova-FarmersDelight?

A port of the popular cooking and farming mod "Farmer's Delight" to "Nova", a plugin platform for Paper servers. No client mod (such as a modded launcher) is needed; you play with regular Minecraft Java Edition.

What's been added

Cooking

Make dishes with effects, like sandwiches, salads, stews and desserts. Pots and skillets let you prepare food for several people at once.

Tools for gathering and storing ingredients

New tools make harvesting and prep more efficient, and there are ways to store ingredients well.

Kitchen and farm decoration blocks

Blocks for decorating kitchens and farms have been added too.

New crops

Four new crops: tomatoes, onions, cabbage and rice. Rice is planted near water. Plant them in a field, harvest when grown, and use them in cooking.

Cutting board

A workstation for cutting ingredients.

  1. 1

    Get a knife

    Craft a special "knife". Besides using it on the cutting board, right-clicking crops with it harvests them quickly without losing seeds.

  2. 2

    Put ingredients on the cutting board

    Place a cutting board and right-click to put raw meat, vegetables and so on on it.

  3. 3

    Cut with a knife

    Right-click with a knife to cut the ingredients (e.g. beef into beef slices, bread and ham into a sandwich).

Cooking pot

A pot that makes stew or soup for several people at once.

  1. 1

    Place a pot and add ingredients

    Right-click to add meat, vegetables, a water bucket and so on. The dish depends on the combination, such as beef stew or chicken soup.

  2. 2

    Heat it from below

    Heat the pot from below with a campfire or similar to start cooking.

  3. 3

    Serve into bowls

    Right-click with an empty bowl to serve one portion. You can also carry the whole pot home.

Other tools and blocks

SkilletA cooking tool used over a campfire that cooks one serving on the spot. Makes fried eggs and more
StoveA multi-slot stove that cooks faster than a furnace. Also works as a light
BasketA storage block for keeping harvested ingredients tidy
Rope / canvasClimbable rope and cloth blocks that brighten up kitchens and farms. Decorative

How to play, in short

There are no special commands. Gather materials, craft, and place tools and blocks with normal survival controls.

This page is based on how the original "Farmer's Delight" works. Depending on server settings, recipes and details may differ from the original. If you notice anything while playing, let us know on Discord.
