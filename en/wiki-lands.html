LANDS

Protecting land & founding nations

Claim chunks with /claim, invite friends, and grow your nation bit by bit.

Claiming land

The most important plugin for protecting your land and growing your town or nation.

  1. 1

    Claim a chunk

    Run /claim to create a land in your name and protect the chunk (16×16 blocks) you're standing in. To choose a name, use /lands create <name> and then /land <land> claim.

  2. 2

    Grow bit by bit

    Use /land <land> claim radius <radius> to claim the area around you at once, or turn on /land <land> claim auto to claim as you walk.

  3. 3

    Invite friends

    Use /land <land> member add <player> to add other players as members of your land.

The command format has changed. Most commands now take the form /land <land> ..., where you type the land's name every time. Find <land> in the /lands menu or with /lands list.

Land chat

There's a private chat that only your land's members can see.

/land <land> chatToggle that land's chat mode (while on, everything you say goes there)
/land <land> chat <message>Send a single message to land chat without switching modes

Founding nations and going to war

You can unite several lands into a nation and make alliances or war with other lands and nations. See Diplomacy & war for details.

Commands

/landsOpen the main menu (UI). Basic actions start here
/claimCreate a land in your name and claim the chunk you're standing in
/land <land> unclaimUnclaim the chunk you're standing in
/land <land> unclaim allUnclaim all of that land (asks for confirmation)
/land <land> member add <player>Add a player as a member
/land <land> member remove <player>Remove a player from members
/land <land> member setrole <player> <role>Set a member's role (permissions)
/lands mapOpen a map of claimed areas
/lands viewShow nearby land borders with particles
/lands unstuckIf you're stuck inside a claimed area, move to nearby unclaimed land
/lands listList all lands on the server

Depending on the server version, you may be able to select the land first with /lands edit <land> and then leave out <land>. If a command doesn't work, ask on Discord.
