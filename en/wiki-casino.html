PLAY

Playing at the casino

Slots, high & low, crash and physical roulette. A break from building your nation.

Where to play

Take the portal in the middle of the lobby to the market; the casino is a short walk away. There are no commands to learn: just sit on a chair or press a button to play.

Getting in

Step on the entrance to open a GUI; pay the entry fee to go in. You can only play while you've properly entered.

One-time passEnter without an entry fee for 30 real-world days from purchase
Auto-renew passPaid automatically and renewed every 30 days
Trial pass7 days. Once per person

Buy passes from the pass vending machine (button) inside the venue. Some venues have VIP-only areas.

Slots

  1. Sit on the chair next to a slot machine
  2. Pull the lever to start with a fixed bet
  3. Matching symbols pay out. Three golds trigger "Gold Rush", which can chain free spins

High & Low

  1. Sit on the chair and choose your bet with the middle button
  2. Guess whether the next number is higher or lower with the LOW / HIGH buttons
  3. The more you win, the bigger the payout. Cash out any time with the middle button

Crash

  1. Sit on the chair and choose your bet with the buttons
  2. The multiplier keeps rising
  3. Press the button again to cash out before it crashes (cash out from 1.20x)

Physical roulette

  1. Press the start button to move to the viewing spot
  2. Use the GUI to choose your bet (1,000 / 10,000 / 50,000) and what to bet on
  3. Red/black, odd/even and LOW/HIGH pay 2x, a 4-square block pays 4x, and a single number pays 16x

If a table is in use, you join a queue and the GUI opens when it's your turn. If you don't do anything for a minute, your turn passes to the next person.

If you forget the rules

There's a sign explaining each game next to its table. Click it to see that game's rules.

Player stats

Enter a gamer ID to see that player's stats on the server. Search from the Play page.
