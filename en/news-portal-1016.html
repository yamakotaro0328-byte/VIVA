VIVA Portal (app) is here New

You can now install the official site as an app on your phone or computer. It's called VIVA Portal.

Everything at a glance — whether the server is open, who's on, your own stats and nation, current wars, and time until the resource world reset, all on one screen.

Get alerts — receive alerts "when the server opens" and "when war is declared or begins". You choose which ones you want.

How to install — open VIVA Portal and press "Add to home screen". On iPhone/iPad, use Safari's Share button → "Add to Home Screen"; opening it from the new icon lets you use alerts (iOS 16.4 or later).

Alerts will start working once the server side is ready. We also added how alerts are handled to the Privacy Policy.
