JOBS REBORN

Earn with a job

Mine ore, chop trees, fight mobs. The things you normally do turn straight into money.

How to get a job

Once you have a job, the things you normally do turn into money. Use what you earn to found a nation, in shops, or at the casino.

  1. 1

    Open the job menu

    Run /jobs browse to open the list of jobs you can take right now.

  2. 2

    Choose a job

    Click a job that fits how you play to take it.

  3. 3

    Work and level up

    Doing your job's actions automatically earns money and XP. As you level up, the same actions pay more.

Jobs

There's a limit on how many jobs you can hold at once.

⛏️ MinerMine stone, cobblestone, coal, iron, diamonds and other ores
🪓 WoodcutterChop logs, plant and grow saplings
🌾 FarmerHarvest wheat, crops and sugar cane, and plant seeds
⚔️ HunterDefeat hostile mobs like zombies, skeletons and creepers
🏗️ BuilderPlace blocks. Recommended for builders
🎣 FishermanCatch fish with a fishing rod

Commands

/jobs browseOpen the list of jobs you can take; join from here
/jobs join <job>Take the named job
/jobs leave <job>Quit that job
/jobs statsCheck your jobs, levels and XP to the next level
/jobs info <job>See what a job pays and for what
Think before quitting. If you quit a job with /jobs leave, the level you built up in that job may reset.
