SHOPKEEPERS / VILLAGER MARKET

Trade with villagers

Shops that look like villagers. Two kinds: Shopkeepers for bartering, and Villager Market for market stalls.

There are two kinds

Both are shops that look like villagers. They're set up a little differently.

Both need a special item. It's sold in the lobby, so buy it first.

Shopkeepers — barter shops

Make a shop that trades items for items.

  1. Use the special item bought in the lobby to spawn a villager
  2. Sneak (Shift) and right-click the villager to open the management menu
  3. Set the "required item (price)" and the "item given (product)" and you're done

To remove it, use /shopkeeper remove.

Villager Market — the villager market

Rent your own villager shop and open a stall.

  1. Use the special item bought in the lobby to place a villager in the market (you can also rent an empty one)
  2. Right-click the villager to open its menu
  3. Set the items you want to sell and their prices, and open your stall

How they differ from chest shops

Chest shopsJust a chest. Trades for money (selling and buying)
ShopkeepersNeeds a special item. Item-for-item bartering
Villager MarketNeeds a special item. You rent a spot in the market and open a stall
