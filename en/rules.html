Rules

Rules so everyone can enjoy playing. Please read them before you join.

What's not allowed, and what's welcome

By joining the server, you agree to these rules, the Terms of Service and the Privacy Policy.

Not allowed

  • Using cheat clients or hacks (hack tools, X-Ray, etc.)
  • Malicious griefing or theft inside claimed territory
  • Real-money trading (RMT) or similar outside trades
  • Abuse, slander, or comments that invade someone's privacy
  • Building or leaving machines that put excessive load on the server
  • Advertising other servers or worlds
  • Becoming personal friends with people you met on this server
  • Anything else the team judges to be disruptive

Welcome

  • Founding nations and expanding territory within the rules
  • Declaring war and fighting territorial wars through the proper procedure
  • Economic strategy such as setting tariffs and trading
  • Signing treaties and alliances with other nations
  • Helping beginners and welcoming them into your town
  • Joining build contests and events
The griefing ban applies inside claimed territory. Builds outside territory aren't protected, so protect anything important with Lands.

If you see a violation

Players can open a vote on the spot.

  1. 1

    Run /report

    /r works too. It opens a vote to remove a player.

  2. 2

    The vote result is applied

    Depending on the result, the player receives a temporary ban (tmpban) or a permanent ban.

  3. 3

    Send evidence in a Discord ticket

    Final penalties are decided by the team. To help us decide, please send screenshots in a Discord ticket whenever possible.

Getting support

If you need help from the team, for example after griefing, a Discord ticket is required.

Open a ticket on Discord and include what happened, where (coordinates), when, and screenshots. We can't handle requests made any other way, such as in-game chat or private messages.
