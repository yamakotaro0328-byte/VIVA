ENGLISH GUIDE

Welcome to VIVA-MC

A Japanese nation-building Minecraft server. Found a nation, run its economy and laws, make allies — or declare war. Java and Bedrock players play together, and joining is free.

Server info

Click the address to copy it.

Address
Java EditionVersion 26.2 · port 25565
Bedrock EditionPort 19132 (phones, tablets, Windows)
PriceFree
LanguageJapanese. English speakers are welcome — feel free to ask in English on Discord.

How to join

Java Edition (PC)

  • Launch Minecraft Java Edition 26.2.
  • Multiplayer → Add Server.
  • Server Address: viva-mc.net (no port needed).
  • Select VIVA-MC and click Join Server.

Bedrock (phone, tablet, Windows)

  • Update Minecraft and tap Play.
  • Open the Servers tab → Add Server.
  • Address: viva-mc.net, Port: 19132.
  • Save, then join from the list.

On Switch, PlayStation or Xbox you can't add servers from the menu. Ask us on Discord and we'll tell you a way that works right now.

What you can do

Nations

Claim land, found a nation

Protect chunks with /claim, invite friends, then unite your lands with /nations create.

Economy

Jobs and shops

Earn money by mining, farming or fighting with /jobs browse, and open chest shops to trade.

Diplomacy

Allies and wars

Form alliances, name enemies and declare war with /land <name> war declare.

Fun

Casino, vehicles, events

Slots, roulette and more in the lobby market, plus vehicles and regular events.

Rules in short

This is a summary. The Japanese rules page is the official version.

Not allowed

  • Griefing or stealing inside someone's claimed territory
  • Cheats and hacked clients such as X-ray (instant ban)
  • Lag machines
  • Real-money trading of in-game items or currency
  • Harassment and hate speech

Good to know

  • Protect your builds with Lands — unclaimed areas are not protected.
  • Need help (e.g. griefing)? Open a ticket on Discord.
  • The resource world resets on the 15th of every month.
  • Money shown as $ is in-game currency only.
